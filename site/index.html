VEILVOICE

VeilVoice removes the biometric voiceprint from a recording and keeps the words. Pitch, formants, timbre and accent are destroyed on your own machine, fully offline, so the speech can still be transcribed but not traced back to a voice.

The website is moving to this address. Until the move is finished, the full site, with downloads, the manual and the roadmap, is at tilas01.github.io/veilvoice.
